# Spellings that are grammar, not vocabulary — excluded from the bundled
# Wortschatz decks with `-exclude`.
#
# Every entry here is a word Wiktionary files as a noun, adjective or verb whose
# frequency rank really belongs to a particle, adverb, interjection or auxiliary
# form spelled the same. The card the builder would make is metalinguistic: "Ja
# -> a yes", "Habe -> belongings", "Würde -> dignity". Teaching those as
# vocabulary teaches the learner nothing they cannot already read.
#
# Matching is case-insensitive on the card's front, so `ja` also drops `Ja`.
# This list is deliberately short and hand-picked: the rule that would generate
# it ("the lowercase spelling is a function word") also buries der Weg, der Tag,
# die Zeit and der Morgen, whose grammar homographs are just as common as they
# are. A word belongs here only when the content reading is the quotation and
# the grammar reading is the usage.
